Slide 3 · Problem Space

The 45-Day Statutory Clock Crisis

Federal IDEA Part C Mandate & The Flat SQL Waitlist Illusion

⚖️ Federal Mandate (34 CFR § 303.310)

States have strictly 45 calendar days from referral to evaluate an infant and deliver an Individualized Family Service Plan (IFSP). Early intervention at 20 months rewires neuroplasticity; waiting until kindergarten misses critical developmental windows.

⏳ 36-Month Age-Out Cliff

Children permanently age out of IDEA Part C at 36 months (age 3). An administrative delay of 45+ days permanently disqualifies a 30-month-old from early intervention services with zero safety net.

🔍 The Flat SQL Waitlist Illusion

Standard SQL sorts providers by waitlist size. Backlogged evaluation centers (waitlist = 75) are merely downstream symptoms. The true drivers are low-wait "Bounce Hubs" whose fax handoffs trigger multi-hop failures.

📉 Cascading Multi-Hop Breaches

When Spanish-language credentials or Medicaid enrollment are missing, toddlers bounce 3 to 4 times—pushing elapsed time to 70–75 days. High-poverty, zero-vehicle neighborhoods (e.g. Bronx NY: 73.2% zero-vehicle) suffer the worst delays.

Slide 4 · Solution

Our Agent — Conversational Multi-Hop Routing Assistant

Three Functional Tracks, Variable-Length ISO GQL & Measurable Clinical Impact

🎯 Track 1: Active Triage

Coordinator inputs an incoming toddler (e.g., 22-month-old Spanish-speaking Medicaid referral in Bronx tract 36005018900). Agent routes through a verified compliant pathway in 22 days (saving 53 days) while enforcing zero-vehicle transit limits.

🔎 Track 2: Quarterly Network Audit

Traverses variable-length ISO GQL paths (-{2,4}->) to expose hidden bounce hubs (like NY-012 & MS-011) that report deceptive 5-day queues yet cause 70+ day breaches across the region.

📍 Track 3: Community Screening

Combines Census ACS poverty, zero-vehicle data, and HRSA NSCH rates to place mobile screening clinics strictly where downstream <=45-day capacity is mathematically confirmed.

🌟 Concrete Clinical Impact Proved by Counterfactual Simulation:

• 1,168 Child-Days Saved: Replacing paper faxing with electronic intake at intermediary bounce hubs recovers 1,168 child-days across the network.
• Age-Out Prevention: Children aged 30+ months are automatically fast-tracked before falling off the 36-month eligibility cliff.
• Compassionate Bilingual Caregiver Notes: Produces plain-language English and Spanish action guides explaining next steps without clinical jargon.
